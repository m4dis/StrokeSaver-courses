Milliseid andmeid rakendus kasutab

  • Asukoht. Rakendus kasutab telefoni ja kella GPS-i, et näidata kaugusi lipuni, griini servadeni ja sihtpunktideni. Asukohaandmed töödeldakse seadmes ja neid ei saadeta kuhugi. Ringi marsruut salvestatakse ainult siis, kui lülitad sisse Health'i treeningu salvestamise.
  • Liikumisandur (Motion & Fitness). Kasutatakse ainult selleks, et tuvastada paigalseisu ja lasta GPS-il puhata (aku sääst). Andmeid ei salvestata.
  • Health (valikuline). Apple Watchil jookseb ring golfitreeninguna, et GPS töötaks käsi all. Kui lubad, salvestab rakendus treeningu (aeg, kalorid, marsruut) Apple Health'i. Need andmed jäävad Health'i ja Apple'i reeglite alla; rakendus ei loe Health'ist midagi muud.
  • Skoorid, märkmed, kepikott, kaaslaste nimed. Salvestatakse ainult sinu seadme andmebaasi. Kustuta kõik andmed: Seaded → Andmed.
  • Väljakupaketid. Laaditakse alla avalikust hoidlast (GitHub). Allalaadimisel näeb GitHub sinu IP-aadressi nagu iga veebiserver; rakendus ise ei saada identifikaatoreid.
  • Ostud ja tellimused. Toimuvad Apple'i App Store'i kaudu. Rakendus ei näe sinu maksevahendit; ostu olek loetakse Apple'ilt.
  • Diagnostika. Krahhi- ja aku-logi salvestub ainult seadmesse. Seda saadetakse edasi ainult siis, kui ise ekspordid ja jagad.

Data the app uses

  • Location. The app uses the phone's and watch's GPS to show distances to the pin, green edges and aim points. Location is processed on the device and never sent anywhere. The round's route is stored only if you enable saving the round to Health.
  • Motion & Fitness. Used only to detect standing still so GPS can rest (battery). Nothing is stored.
  • Health (optional). On Apple Watch the round runs as a golf workout so GPS keeps working with your wrist down. If you allow it, the app saves the workout (time, calories, route) to Apple Health. That data stays in Health under Apple's rules; the app reads nothing else from Health.
  • Scores, notes, club bag, playing partners' names. Stored only in the database on your device. Delete everything: Settings → Data.
  • Course packages. Downloaded from a public repository (GitHub). GitHub sees your IP address as any web server does; the app itself sends no identifiers.
  • Purchases and subscriptions. Handled by Apple's App Store. The app never sees your payment method; purchase status is read from Apple.
  • Diagnostics. Crash and battery logs are stored on the device only and leave it only if you export and share them yourself.

What the app does NOT do: no account required, no analytics or advertising trackers, no selling or sharing of data, no processing outside your device (except course downloads and Apple purchases).
