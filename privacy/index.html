Data the app uses

  • Location. The app uses the phone's and watch's GPS to show distances to the pin, green edges and aim points. Location is processed on the device and is not sent anywhere, except when you yourself send a course report (see the addendum below) or use a temporary flag in a cross-country game, which is saved with the round. The round's route is stored only if you enable saving the round to Health.
  • Motion & Fitness. Used only to detect standing still so GPS can rest (battery). Nothing is stored.
  • Health (optional). On Apple Watch the round runs as a golf workout so GPS keeps working with your wrist down. If you allow it, the app saves the workout (time, calories, route) to Apple Health. That data stays in Health under Apple's rules; the app reads nothing else from Health.
  • Scores, notes, club bag, playing partners' names. Stored in the database on your device; synced to your account only if you sign in. Delete everything: Settings → Data.
  • Course packages. Downloaded from a public repository (GitHub). GitHub sees your IP address as any web server does; the app itself sends no identifiers.
  • Purchases and subscriptions. Handled by Apple's App Store. The app never sees your payment method; purchase status is read from Apple.
  • Diagnostics. Crash and battery logs are stored on the device only and leave it only if you export and share them yourself.

What the app does NOT do: no account required (an account is optional, see the addenda below), no analytics or advertising trackers, no selling of data. Without an account and without the optional switches, data is processed only on your device (except course downloads and Apple purchases). Account sync, games and friends, Custom rules (AI), pace sharing and course reports are described in the addenda below.

Data sources: orthophoto and 1 m elevation model © Estonian Land and Spatial Development Board; holes and greens © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL).

Children. The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from them.

Changes. This policy is updated before any new data processing is added; the date is at the top and dated addenda are at the end.

Täiendus 12.10.2026: kontod, mängud, sõbrad ja AI-reeglid (ET)

Konto ja sünkroonimine. Konto loomisel salvestame e-posti aadressi ja sisselogimise ajatemplid (Supabase Auth, server EL-is, Iirimaa). Kontoga sünkrooniv sisu: ringid ja skoorid, märkmed, kepikott, kaaslaste nimed ja HCP-d, mängu formaadid. Sünkroonimine toimub ainult sisselogituna; ilma kontota jäävad andmed telefoni. Konto ja kõik pilves olevad andmed saab kustutada äpist (Seaded → Konto → Kustuta konto); kustutamine on lõplik.

Mängud ja sõbrad. Kui lood või liitud mänguga (liitumislink või kood), näevad sama mängu liikmed sinu nime, HCP-d, tiid ja selle mängu skoore. Profiili otsinimi (handle) ja nimi on nähtavad teistele kasutajatele otsingus; HCP on nähtav ainult kinnitatud sõpradele ja sama mängu liikmetele; ringide skoore näevad ainult sama mängu liikmed selle mängu ulatuses. Mängu liitujale ilma kontota loome anonüümse konto, mis kustutatakse 30 päeva pärast viimase mängu lõppu, kui e-posti ei lisatud.

Oma reeglid (AI). Kui kirjeldad mängureegleid vabas vormis, saadame kirjelduse meie serveri kaudu Anthropic PBC (USA) Claude'i mudelile tõlkimiseks formaadi kirjelduseks. Saadame ainult reegliteksti ja mängijate HCP-d; nimesid ei saadeta. Anthropic ei kasuta API kaudu saadetud sisu mudelite treenimiseks. Reeglite dikteerimisel kasutab äpp Apple'i kõnetuvastust (Speech), mis võib heli töödelda Apple'i serverites Apple'i privaatsustingimuste järgi; tekst jõuab meieni alles pärast tuvastamist. Reegliteksti me ei salvesta kauemaks kui tõlkimiseks vaja; valminud formaat salvestatakse sinu kontole.

Väljakute andmeallikad. Eesti väljakute ortofoto ja kõrgusmudel: Maa- ja Ruumiamet. Lõuna-Aafrika pilootväljakud: City of Cape Town Open Data Portal (ortofoto), Copernicus GLO-30 (kõrgusmudel), OpenStreetMap kaastöötajad (ODbL). Andmeallikad on näha iga väljaku lehel.

Addendum 12 October 2026: accounts, games, friends and AI rules (EN)

Account and sync. When you create an account we store your e-mail address and sign-in timestamps (Supabase Auth, hosted in the EU, Ireland). Synced content: rounds and scores, notes, your bag, companions' names and handicaps, game formats. Sync only runs while signed in; without an account everything stays on your phone. You can delete the account and all cloud data from the app (Settings → Account → Delete account); deletion is final.

Games and friends. When you create or join a game (link or code), members of that game see your name, handicap, tee and that game's scores. Your handle and name are visible to other users in search; your handicap only to accepted friends and members of the same game; round scores only to members of that game, for that game. A player who joins without an account gets an anonymous account, deleted 30 days after their last game ends unless an e-mail is added.

Custom rules (AI). When you describe game rules in free text, our server sends the description to Anthropic PBC (USA), the Claude model, to translate it into a format definition. We send only the rule text and players' handicaps, never names. Anthropic does not train on API content. If you dictate rules, the app uses Apple's speech recognition, which may process audio on Apple's servers under Apple's privacy terms; we receive only the recognised text. We keep the rule text no longer than needed for translation; the resulting format is saved to your account.

Course data sources. Estonian courses: Estonian Land and Spatial Development Board orthophoto and elevation model. South African pilot courses: City of Cape Town Open Data Portal (orthophoto), Copernicus GLO-30 (elevation), OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL). Sources are shown on each course page.

Tempoandmed ja raportid väljakule (lisatud 15.10.2026, ET). Kui lubad tempoandmete jagamise (Seaded → Privaatsus, vaikimisi väljas), saadab äpp iga mängitud raja alguse ja lõpu aja koos väljaku, rajanumbri ja flaidi suurusega, ilma sinu nime, konto ja skoorita. Väljak näeb neid ainult koondatuna (vähemalt viie ringi kaupa) ega seo neid stardiaegade ega broneeringutega. Andmed kustutatakse 24 kuu pärast. Väljakule saadetud raportid (tekst, asukoht ja foto, millest on eemaldatud foto metaandmed) on klubile nähtavad sinu nimeta ning kustutatakse 12 kuud pärast lahendamist.

Pace data and course reports (added 15 October 2026, EN). If you allow pace sharing (Settings → Privacy, off by default), the app sends the start and end time of each hole you play, with the course, hole number and group size, but without your name, account or score. The course sees these only in aggregate (at least five rounds at a time) and never links them to tee times or bookings. They are deleted after 24 months. Reports you send to a course (text, location and a photo stripped of its metadata) are visible to the club without your name and are deleted 12 months after they are resolved.

Mängud ja külalised, kohtunikukutse, kaardi kinnitus (lisatud 18.10.2026, ET). Mängu andmed (nimed, HCP, sugu, tii, skoorid) hoitakse korraldaja mängu all ja neid näevad ainult selle mängu liikmed. Mänguga saab liituda ka ilma äpita veebilehel liitumislingi kaudu: külalise nimi ja soovi korral HCP salvestatakse mängu alla, kus neid näevad korraldaja ja mängijad; külalise skoorid sisestab tema flaidi marker. Säilitus: külalise andmed kustutatakse 30 päeva pärast mängu lõppu; anonüümsed kontod 30 päeva pärast viimast mängu; korraldajata jäänud mängud 30 päeva pärast lõppu. Konto kustutamisel kustutatakse kohe sinu koht ja sinu sisestatud skoorid mängudes; sinu korraldatud pooleli mängud tühistatakse kõigile liikmetele ja sinu eesnimi eemaldatakse sinu korraldatud mängudest, mängud ise jäävad teistele liikmetele alles. Liitumise veebileht ei kasuta küpsiseid ega brauseri salvestust ega tee päringuid kolmandatele osapooltele; kuritarvituse piiramiseks hoitakse külastaja IP-aadressi soolatud räsi kuni üks päev, aadressi ennast mitte. Võistlusmängus saab mängija kutsuda kohtuniku: klubile saadetakse rada, asukoht, GPS täpsus, võistleja avalik nimi (nt „Mari M.") ja lühike märkus; nime, kontot ega skoore ei saadeta. Kaardi kinnitus (mängija ja markeri kinnitus pärast viimast rada) salvestatakse mängu juurde ajatemplina ja on nähtav sama mängu liikmetele ning võistluse korraldanud klubile koos skooridega.

Games and guests, referee call, card confirmation (added 18 October 2026, EN). A game's data (names, handicap, gender, tee, scores) is held under the organiser's game and is visible only to that game's members. A game can also be joined without the app, on the web page behind the join link: the guest's name and optional handicap are stored under the game, where the organiser and the players see them; the guest's scores are entered by their flight's marker. Retention: guest data is deleted 30 days after the game ended; anonymous accounts 30 days after their last game; games whose organiser is gone 30 days after they ended. When you delete your account, your place and the scores you entered in games are deleted at once; unfinished games you organise are cancelled for all members, and your first name is removed from the games you organised, which stay with the other members. The join web page uses no cookies, no browser storage and no third-party requests; to limit abuse, a salted hash of the visitor's IP address is kept for at most one day, never the address itself. In a competition game a player can call a referee: the club receives the hole, location, GPS accuracy, the player's public entry name (e.g. "Mari M.") and a short note; no name, account or scores. Card confirmation (the player's and the marker's confirmation after the last hole) is stored with the game as a timestamp and is visible to the game's members and, with the scores, to the club that organised the competition.
